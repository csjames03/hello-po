# Your stay

## Check-in and check-out

Check-in is from 2 PM. Check-out is until 12 noon. The front desk is open 24 hours, so late arrivals are welcome. Guests show a valid government ID or passport at check-in.

## Early check-in

Early check-in from 11 AM is free if the room is ready. Guests who arrive earlier can leave their bags at the front desk and use the pool, restaurant and showers while they wait.

## Late check-out

Late check-out until 3 PM costs 1,500 pesos and depends on availability. Ask the front desk on the morning of departure. Guests can store luggage at the front desk after check-out for free.

## Rooms and beds

There are four room types. The Garden Room has a queen bed and fits 2 guests. The Ocean View Room has a king bed and a balcony and fits 3 guests. The Family Suite has two bedrooms and fits 5 guests. The Beachfront Villa has a private plunge pool and fits 4 guests. Every room has air conditioning, a rain shower, a minibar, a safe and free Wi-Fi.

## Extra beds and children

An extra bed costs 1,200 pesos a night and is available in the Ocean View Room, Family Suite and Beachfront Villa. Children under 6 stay free when they share the parents' bed. Baby cots are free on request.

## Breakfast

A breakfast buffet is included with every booking. It is served at the Alon restaurant from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM.

## Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi covers all rooms and common areas. The network is Isla Azul Guest and the password is on the key card holder.
