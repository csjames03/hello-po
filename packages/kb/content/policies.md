# Policies

## Cancellation

Cancellation is free up to 7 days before arrival. After that, the first night is charged. A no-show is charged for the full stay.

## Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to arrive.

## Deposits and payment

Guests can pay in full when booking, or pay a 50 percent deposit and the balance at check-in. The resort accepts major credit and debit cards, GCash and cash.

## Changing a booking

Dates can be changed for free up to 7 days before arrival, subject to availability. Changes after that are handled by the reservations team.

## Pets

Pets are not allowed. Service animals are welcome.

## Smoking

All rooms are non-smoking. Smoking is allowed only in the marked outdoor areas. Smoking in a room carries a 5,000 peso cleaning fee.

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours are from 10 PM to 7 AM.

## Visitors

Day visitors are welcome at the restaurant and pool bar. Overnight visitors must be registered at the front desk.

## Lost and found

Lost items are kept at the front desk for 60 days.
