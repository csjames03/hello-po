# Activities and getting here

## Location

Isla Azul Beach Resort is on the beach in El Nido, Palawan, in the Philippines.

## Weather and best time to visit

The dry season runs from November to May and is the best time for island hopping. June to October is the rainy season, with more boat tour cancellations. Daytime temperatures are around 27 to 32 degrees Celsius all year.

## Kayak rental

Kayaks cost 500 pesos an hour, from 7 AM to 5 PM, at the beach hut. Life jackets are included.

## Snorkeling

Snorkel gear is free for guests at the beach hut.

## Island hopping tours

Island hopping tours run daily from 9 AM to 4 PM and cost 1,800 pesos per person, including lunch and snorkel gear. Book by 8 PM the day before at the front desk. Tours can be cancelled by the boat operator in bad weather, with a full refund.

## Sunset cruise

A sunset cruise runs at 4:30 PM and costs 1,200 pesos per person, including drinks.

## Airport transfers

A private van transfer from El Nido Airport costs 800 pesos per person and takes about 20 minutes. A shared van from Puerto Princesa costs 1,500 pesos per person and takes 5 to 6 hours. Book transfers at least 48 hours before arrival.

## Environmental fee

The town of El Nido charges an eco-tourism fee of 200 pesos per guest. It is paid locally and is not included in the room price.
