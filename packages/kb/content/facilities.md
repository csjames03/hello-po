# Facilities

## Amenities overview

The resort has an infinity pool, the Alon beachfront restaurant, a pool bar, room service, a spa, a 24 hour gym, a kids club, free Wi-Fi, free parking, laundry service, and kayak rental and free snorkel gear at the beach hut.

## Restaurant

The Alon beachfront restaurant is open from 6 AM to 10 PM. It serves Filipino and international dishes, with vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options. Dinner reservations are recommended on weekends.

## Pool bar

The pool bar serves drinks and light snacks from 10 AM to 10 PM. Happy hour is from 5 PM to 7 PM.

## Room service

Room service is available from 6 AM to 11 PM.

## Infinity pool

The infinity pool is open from 7 AM to 9 PM. Pool towels are provided. There is no lifeguard, so children under 12 must be with an adult.

## Spa

The spa is open from 9 AM to 9 PM. Massages start at 2,500 pesos for 60 minutes. Book at the front desk or by calling the resort. Booking a day ahead is recommended.

## Gym

The gym is open 24 hours for guests, with a key card.

## Kids club

The kids club is free for children aged 4 to 12, from 9 AM to 5 PM, with supervised games and crafts.

## Parking

Parking is free for guests.

## Laundry

Laundry service is available, with same-day return for items handed in before 10 AM. Prices are listed in the room.

## Medical help

The front desk keeps a first aid kit, and a doctor can be called 24 hours. The nearest hospital is a 20 minute drive.
